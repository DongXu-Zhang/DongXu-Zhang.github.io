# Dongxu Zhang

I am a master's student in Software Engineering at Xi'an Jiaotong University, advised by [Prof. Jihua Zhu](https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/web/zhujh). I will join Tsinghua University in September 2027 to pursue a Ph.D. under the supervision of [Prof. Chang Qiao](https://faculty.dpi.tsinghua.edu.cn/qiaochang/zh_CN/index.htm).

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Taiyuan University of Technology, where I was advised by [Prof. Juanjuan Zhao](https://rjxy.tyut.edu.cn/info/1191/3989.htm). I previously visited Shanghai AI Laboratory, working with [Cheng Tan](https://chengtan9907.github.io/), and was a research intern with [Ning Yang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=b4cE0J4AAAAJ) at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research interests include efficient and reliable reasoning in large language models, multimodal learning, and AI for scientific discovery. My earlier work focused on 3D vision and point cloud registration.
