**🚀I am now working on LLM Reasoning and Chain-of-Thought (CoT). Currently, I am actively seeking Ph.D. opportunities for the Fall 2027 intake.** I am particularly interested in advancing LLM Reasoning and Multimodal Learning. If you are looking for a self-motivated student with a strong background in 3D-Vision and LLM, or if you are interested in academic collaboration, I would be honored to discuss further via email at [zhangdongxu@stu.xjtu.edu.cn](mailto:zhangdongxu@stu.xjtu.edu.cn).


# 🔥 News
[Apr. 2026] 🎉 Excited to share that our paper "**Chain-of-Thought Compression Should Not Be Blind: V-Skip for Efficient Multimodal Reasoning via Dual-Path Anchoring**" has been accepted to **ACL 2026** as a **Main Conference Oral** presentation! We propose **V-Skip** to address "Visual Amnesia" in MLLM reasoning compression. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13879) \| [project page](https://dongxu-zhang.github.io/v-skip.github.io/).

[Mar. 2026] 🎉 Excited to share that our two papers "**PointRFT: Explicit Reinforcement Fine-tuning for Point Cloud Perception**" and "**PersonalQ: Select, Quantize, and Serve Personalized Diffusion Models for Efficient Inference**" have been accepted by **ICME 2026**!

[Mar. 2026] 🔥 Excited to share our new preprint "**PointCoT: A Multi-modal Benchmark for Explicit 3D Geometric Reasoning**" on arXiv! We propose a novel **Look–Think–Answer** paradigm that empowers MLLMs with geometry-grounded Chain-of-Thought reasoning over 3D point clouds, significantly reducing geometric hallucinations. Check out the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23945) and [project page](https://dongxu-zhang.github.io/PointCoT.github.io/)!
